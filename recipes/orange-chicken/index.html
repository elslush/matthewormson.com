Orange chicken

Crisp chicken thighs with a sweet orange glaze.

Adapted from Jason Farmer’s recipe

Two meals from one prep. Prepare 4 portions of chicken; finish half with the listed sauce for 2 servings. Freeze the other half for another day. Infusions and Basic sauce also make extra.

Make ahead

1. Mix the ginger–garlic infusion

Mince the garlic and grate the ginger. Combine with the oil and water; rest for 15 minutes in the refrigerator.

For this step1 tbsp garlic; 2 tsp fresh ginger; 1 tsp vegetable oil; 1 tsp water.

Hot oil · 275°F / 135°C

2. Make the chili infusion

Moisten the flakes with the water in a large heatproof bowl. Heat the oil to 275°F / 135°C. Pour it in slowly; the mixture foams. Refrigerate both infusions and use within 4 days, or freeze.

For this step¼ cup crushed red pepper flakes; 2 tbsp water; 2 tbsp vegetable oil.

Makes extra

3. Make the Basic sauce

Warm everything except the xanthan gum until dissolved, without boiling. Remove from the heat; whisk in the gum.

For this step2 tbsp Chinese light soy sauce; 2 tbsp granulated sugar; 2 tbsp dark corn syrup; 2 ¼ tsp Morton table salt; ¾ tsp MSG; ⅛ tsp white pepper; ½ tsp ground ginger; 2 tbsp water; 1/16 tsp xanthan gum.

For today’s half-batch

4. Mix the orange sauce

Whisk the orange sauce ingredients together until smooth. Stir again immediately before adding to the wok.

For this step4 ½ tbsp granulated sugar; 4 ½ tbsp distilled white vinegar; 1 ½ tbsp prepared basic sauce; 1 ½ tbsp water; ⅛ tsp citric acid; 2 ¼ tsp cornstarch; 1/16 tsp xanthan gum; ¼ tsp pure orange extract.

Refrigerate 30 minutes–4 hours

5. Marinate the chicken

Cut the thighs into 1-inch pieces. Coat with the marinade ingredients; cover and refrigerate.

For this step1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs; 1 tbsp dried whole egg powder; ½ tsp Morton table salt; ¼ tsp white pepper; 1 tbsp cornstarch; 1 tsp vegetable oil.

Use a separate bowl

6. Mix the dry dredge

Combine the dry dredge ingredients.

For this step⅓ cup cornstarch; Scant ⅓ cup potato starch; ¼ tsp onion powder; ¼ tsp garlic powder; ⅛ tsp white pepper.

Keep cold

7. Mix the wet batter

Whisk the batter ingredients together. Set the bowl over ice to keep it cold.

For this step1 cup all-purpose flour; ⅔ cup cornstarch; Scant ½ cup potato starch; 1 tsp onion powder; 1 tsp garlic powder; ¼ tsp white pepper; ½ tsp baking powder; ½ tsp Morton table salt; 3 tbsp dried whole egg powder; 1 ⅓ cups ice water.

First fry · 350°F / 175°C

8. Coat and cook the chicken

Coat pieces in dry dredge, then wet batter. Fry in small batches at 350°F / 175°C until the thickest pieces reach 165°F / 74°C. This cook-through step replaces the source’s partial first fry.

For this stepAs needed vegetable oil.

At least 6 hours in the freezer

9. Chill, freeze, and divide

Drain, then refrigerate promptly in a shallow single layer; do not wait for room temperature. Get it into the refrigerator or freezer within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Freeze in a single layer for at least 6 hours. Bag half for another meal.

Second fry · 350°F / 175°C

10. Crisp half of the chicken

Keep half frozen for another meal. Fry the other half straight from frozen, in small batches, at 350°F / 175°C until crisp and 165°F / 74°C inside. The source suggests about 7 minutes; use temperature as your guide.

Have everything beside the wok

11. Heat the aromatics and sauce

Heat the wok oil. Stir-fry the two infusions for 10–20 seconds; add the wine, then all of the stirred orange sauce. Bring to a boil until thickened.

For this step1–2 tsp vegetable oil; 1 tsp prepared ginger–garlic infusion; ¼ tsp prepared chili infusion; 1 tsp Shaoxing cooking wine.

Finish in 30–45 seconds

12. Glaze and serve

Toss in the crisp chicken until coated. Finish with sesame oil and serve with rice.

For this step¼ tsp toasted sesame oil.

Source & notes

Ingredients and cooking technique adapted from Jason Farmer’s orange chicken recipe. Visit his page for the original recipe, photographs, and video. This adaptation has not been tested in Matthew’s kitchen.

The method here cooks the meat through before freezing and reheats it to 165°F / 74°C. USDA guidance advises cooking meat and poultry completely in one session. See also USDA cooking temperatures and prompt chilling guidance.

For the reserved half of the chicken, make another batch of orange sauce and wok ingredients when ready to finish it. Do not double the sauce and use it all on today’s half.
