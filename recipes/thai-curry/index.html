Thai red chicken curry

Chicken, eggplant, and bamboo shoots in a rich coconut curry. A whole can of red curry paste, plenty of Thai chilies, and rice to catch the sauce.

Prep

1. Get everything ready

Slice the chicken (2 breasts), Chinese eggplant (1 eggplant), red bell pepper (½ cup), and Thai chilies (About 6, to taste). Drain the bamboo shoots (1 cup) and grate the palm sugar (1 tbsp). Set out the basil, lime leaves, and seasonings. Start your rice according to its package or rice-cooker directions.

⤢
Look for this

Have the vegetables and seasonings ready before the curry paste hits the pan.

Medium heat

2. Start with the coconut cream

Open the coconut milk (2 × 400 mL cans) and scoop the thick cream from the top into a large, deep pan. Add the torn makrut lime leaves (6–8 leaves). Heat gently, stirring, until the cream bubbles and thickens. Keep the rest of the coconut milk for later.

⤢
Look for this

The cream goes from a loose puddle to a thicker, bubbling base.

3 more photos
Stir frequently

3. Cook the curry paste

Add 1 whole can of red curry paste to the reduced coconut cream. Stir and cook until fragrant, well combined, and noticeably thicker. Keep it moving so the paste does not catch on the pan.

⤢
Look for this

A spatula drawn through the paste leaves a visible trail.

2 more photos
Build the sauce

4. Add chilies, eggplant, and coconut milk

Stir in the sliced Thai chilies (About 6, to taste). Add the rounds of Chinese eggplant (1 eggplant) and all the remaining coconut milk (from 2 × 400 mL cans total). Stir until the paste is evenly dispersed and the eggplant is coated in sauce.

⤢
Look for this

The thick curry base loosens into a smooth orange sauce.

2 more photos
Gentle bubbling

5. Let the sauce reduce

Bring the sauce to a steady bubble, then adjust the heat so it cooks without scorching. Let it reduce slightly and give the eggplant time to soften, stirring occasionally. Allow roughly 10–20 minutes and use the texture as your guide.

6. Add chicken and the final ingredients

Lower the heat to a simmer. Add 2 breasts of sliced chicken, 1 cup of bamboo shoots, ½ cup of red bell pepper, ½ cup of Thai basil, 1 tbsp of grated palm sugar, 2 tsp of fish sauce, and 1 tbsp of tamarind concentrate. Stir everything into the sauce so the chicken cooks evenly.

⤢
Look for this

The chicken and vegetables should sit in the sauce, rather than on top of it.

1 more photo
Check doneness

7. Simmer until the chicken is cooked

Cover and simmer gently, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through and the eggplant is tender. Start checking after about 10–15 minutes; larger pieces may need longer. Check the thickest chicken pieces with a food thermometer: they must reach 165°F / 74°C. Taste the finished sauce and adjust to your preference.

Ready to eat

8. Spoon over rice

Remove the whole lime leaves and any tough basil stems. Spoon the curry over hot cooked rice, with plenty of sauce, chicken, and vegetables in each bowl.

⤢

Chicken should reach 165°F / 74°C in its thickest part. USDA temperature guidance.
